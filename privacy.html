Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Hunter's Home Services ("I," "me") provides snow removal, car detailing and window cleaning in the Coralville, North Liberty and Iowa City, Iowa area. This page explains what information I collect and how I use it.

What I collect

When you text or call me, fill out a form (including Facebook or Instagram lead forms), or book a service, I may collect your name, phone number, email, service address, vehicle details and the service you want.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, give you a quote and schedule your service
  • To do the job and send you a bill
  • To follow up about future service, which you can opt out of anytime

Sharing

I do not sell or rent your information. I only share it when needed to run the business (for example, payment apps like Venmo or Cash App when you pay) or if required by law.

Ads and social media

I may advertise on Facebook and Instagram. If you submit a lead form there, Meta shares the information you entered with me. Meta's own use of your data is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting your info

I keep your info only as long as I need it to serve you. To have it updated or deleted, or to stop getting messages, just text or call me.
